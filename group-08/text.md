---
title: In Plain Sight 
authors: Yana Najy, Selena Picardi
---

**Artificial intelligence** is often presented as weightless, yet it runs on material things: minerals[[minerals]] mined into chips, batteries and camera sensors, and the servers, cloud data centers and cables that store and process data. The same material chain that puts AI in our pockets also puts it on the battlefield. A **phone call is not immaterial**: Israel's Unit 8200 stores millions of Palestinians' intercepted calls on commercial cloud servers, where AI systems sort them into bombing targets in Gaza. In September 2025 a UN Commission of Inquiry[[un]] concluded that Israel is committing genocide in Gaza. **In Plain Sight** _traces these materialities_ through 26 documented chains, read from left to right as a path from the hand to the state. It begins with an ordinary phone in your hand, standing in for the everyday devices we carry, whose camera, storage, processor and battery each become the start of a thread. These threads lead to the companies whose technology sits inside the phone, each shown beside the product people know it for, so that a familiar brand is tied to the work it does elsewhere. From there, the threads pass through what they supply, which turns each company into the material form its technology takes and shows that the same infrastructure running our phones also runs military and Israel state systems. The supplies then flow into the systems they feed, grouped by purpose and described by what they do, so the reader sees what the material becomes on the ground. Finally, each system is tied to the Israeli units that operate it, from the army and the Ministry of Defense to civilian authorities, closing the chain from consumer product to state actor. Along the way, threads split and gather: one company can feed several systems, while many threads converge on a few institutions. Where no source identifies a company or a unit, its box is left empty with a dashed outline, so the gaps stay visible. Each thread is colored by how well its link is documented, from A (confirmed by the company or an official source) to D (alleged, single source or denied): none reaches A, 9 are B, 14 are C and 3 are D. The missing evidence is part of the finding, because these supply chains are {#FF0000|BUILT TO STAY OUT OF SIGHT}.
